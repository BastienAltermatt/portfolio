BottleNeck - Monitor business performance

How can management get a consistent view of commercial performance, margins and inventory?

Power BIPower QueryDAXSQLite

The business need

This second BottleNeck study focuses on online wine retail. It combines a review of ways to use the SQLite database with a Power BI report for management and product teams.

≈ €2.44Mreported revenue
≈ €1.15Mreported total margin
46.98%margin ratio used in the report

Figures from the project summary. The margin ratio is total margin divided by revenue, as defined in the report. This portfolio presents a static summary of the case.

Data and scope

Source database
Bottleneck.db, supported by a data dictionary and relational schema. Four tables are used: Web, Finance, Sales and Promo.
Analytical model
Dim_Produit and Dim_Date filter Fact_Sales, Fact_Finance and Fact_Promo in a star schema.
Areas covered
Revenue, margins, units sold and orders, average prices, inventory, stock turnover and promotions.

The approach

  1. Compare three options: a direct SQLite connection, CSV exports and data preparation with Power Query. Select Power BI with transformations centralised in Power Query.
  2. Keep the tables separate and name dimensions and fact tables according to their analytical role.
  3. Split composite keys such as 2022-11_304 to recover the period and SKU, the shared product identifier.
  4. Create a date dimension and relationships from the Product and Date dimensions to the fact tables, so analyses use consistent product and period filters.
  5. Define DAX measures for revenue, margin, units sold, orders, average prices, inventory, turnover and promotional indicators.
  6. Build six pages: executive overview, commercial performance, prices and margins, inventory, promotions and business recommendations.

Deliverables

A documented comparison of technical options and a Power BI file with six business pages, supported by a presentation and speaking notes. The model and transformations explain the architecture choices.

The contribution

The project summary identifies Wine as the dominant segment. Recommendations focus on strong segments, margin protection, reducing excess inventory and targeting promotions more effectively.

Findings and decisions

Performance
Revenue and margin are considered together, so performance is assessed beyond sales volume alone.
Inventory and promotions
Turnover indicators and dedicated report pages help identify products and promotional actions to examine first.
Data period
Finance and Sales also cover October 2023, beyond the September end date stated in the brief. Comparison periods therefore need to be checked by table.

Limitations and perspective

Inventory is a point-in-time measure: aggregation across periods must be interpreted according to the measure used. A sales change during a promotion does not establish a causal effect. Comparisons require consistent periods and indicator definitions.

Reference documents: P9 recommendation report, pages 3 to 8, and the BottleNeck Power BI dashboard, with six business pages and a control page.

Property - Estimate and pre-classify

How can the portfolio be valued as of 31 December 2022 and new opportunities receive an initial classification?

Pythonpandasscikit-learnK-means

The business need

Les Plus Beaux Logis de Paris needs a portfolio valuation and an operational screening tool. The analysis covers 26,196 transactions from 2017 to 2021, a portfolio of 275 assets and 40 opportunities to classify.

7.51%mean absolute percentage error on the test set
€176.5Mestimated value as of 31 December 2022
16 / 40properties with a type reference available

Values saved in the supplied notebook, consistent with the presentation. Evaluation uses a historical chronological test set; the 2022 valuation is a model estimate.

Data and scope

Historical data
26,196 transactions from 2017 to 2021, compared by property type, year and Paris district.
Regression
20,956 older observations for training and 5,240 more recent observations for testing; the final representation contains 22 variables.
Portfolio and opportunities
275 assets to value as of 31 December 2022 and 40 properties to pre-classify using price per square metre.

The approach

  1. Explore changes in price per square metre and the role of location and property type.
  2. Encode postcode and property type as categories, then create interactions with floor area and time.
  3. Train LinearRegression(fit_intercept=False) with an 80/20 chronological split and measure absolute error as a percentage of each property’s actual value.
  4. Apply the same transformations to the portfolio and align columns to estimate each asset’s value as of 31 December 2022.
  5. Run two-cluster K-means on price per square metre, interpreting the centroids rather than the arbitrary cluster numbers.
  6. Check opportunities found in the historical data using four fields: transaction value, floor area, postcode and municipality.

Deliverables

A notebook covering preparation, regression, valuation, K-means and partial validation, plus a 16-slide presentation. The notebook also includes a CSV export of estimated valuations.

The contribution

The approach converts the business relationship value = floor area × price per square metre into interpretable variables. A chronological split provides a test closer to predicting the future from the past than a random mix of periods.

Findings and decisions

Regression evaluation
Mean error is 7.51% and median error is 7.54% on the 5,240 test transactions. The case objective of less than 10% mean error is met.
Portfolio comparison
The Corporate segment accounts for approximately €102.6M across 121 assets; the Individual segment for approximately €74.0M across 154 assets. This supports prioritisation of an asset-by-asset review.
Pre-classification
Each cluster contains 20 properties. Their centres are around €9,807 and €7,409 per square metre. Mapping them to property types requires interpretation.
Partial validation
16 opportunities exactly match historical transactions: 9 apartments and 7 commercial properties. All 16 types agree; the other 24 have no reference label.

VIDEO GAMES · DECISION SUPPORT

UOI Games - Choose a market segment

Which video game segment should be prioritised, and what evidence supports that choice?

PythonpandasExploratory analysisScenarios

The business need

UOI Games is exploring a market direction for a game. The approach combines historical sales, platforms and consoles, questionnaire preferences, ratings and recent supply.

1.40Mcautious scenario · first quartile
2.08Mcentral scenario · median
3.88Mhigh scenario · third quartile

Sales reference points from the presentation, based on 465 titles selling at least one million copies. These are conditional historical values, not a forecast of UOI Games sales.

Data and scope

Organisation
Eight ordered notebooks: exploration, cleaning, historical sales, consoles, questionnaire, ratings, segment selection and recommendation.
Questionnaire
62 respondents: supplementary exploratory evidence, with no established market representativeness.
Decision matrix
Four equally weighted criteria: historical sales, preferences, average 2022 ratings and the number of recent games.

The approach

  1. Organise the analysis into eight notebooks, with documented dependencies and an execution order.
  2. Combine historical sales, platforms and consoles, 62 questionnaire responses, ratings and recent supply.
  3. Compare four equally weighted criteria. For a segment combining several categories, average their contributions rather than adding them, to avoid an artificial advantage.
  4. Convert ranks into points with pandas, then assess genres and platforms in separate matrices.
  5. Aggregate sales of the same title across platforms, select a commercial benchmark of titles selling at least one million copies, and use the first quartile, median and third quartile as historical reference points.

Deliverables

Eight notebooks, a README stating their execution order, a decision matrix and the classement_genres.xlsx output, followed by scenarios and a strategic recommendation.

The contribution

The final presentation recommends a premium AAA action-adventure direction, with a single-player narrative focus, on PC and PlayStation. The proposed next steps are a business case, concept testing and a prototype before an investment decision.

Findings and decisions

Segment selection
The ranking combines different types of evidence. It makes the reasoning inspectable and allows the criteria to be discussed.
Workflow organisation
Separate notebooks improve readability and maintenance but require dependencies and execution order to be respected.
Scope of the scenarios
The quartiles describe the selected reference titles. Marketing budget, studio reputation and competition at launch are not included in this benchmark.
Python excerpt: score the genres

Python · notebook 07, genre comparison

nombre_genres = len(matrice_genres)

for colonne, colonne_points in criteres_genres.items():
    rang = matrice_genres[colonne].rank(
        method="min", ascending=False
    )
    matrice_genres[colonne_points] = nombre_genres + 1 - rang

matrice_genres["score_total"] = matrice_genres[
    list(criteres_genres.values())
].sum(axis=1)

matrice_genres = matrice_genres.sort_values(
    "score_total", ascending=False
).reset_index(drop=True)

The loop ranks each criterion separately. Points are then added without further weighting. For ties, method="min" assigns the best shared rank.

Limitations and perspective

The one-million-copy threshold selects commercial successes and introduces selection bias. The scenarios are not forecasts for a new studio. Matrix weighting is a methodological choice; a larger number of recent games describes supply and does not, by itself, establish a more profitable opportunity.

Reference documents: UOI Games presentation, slides 9 to 16; notebooks 01 to 08 and their README. Notebook 07 details the genre and platform comparisons, averaging grouped categories.

Sanitoral - Track project variances

How can different management levels identify variances and prioritise projects for review?

Power BIPower QueryDAXData modelling

The business need

Sanitoral needs to compare planned and actual results for general, regional and country management. The report must highlight deviations and remain refreshable from new Excel extracts.

104projects monitored
102projects with at least one alert
349distinct phases on alert

Figures from the project summary, using a 15% alert threshold. Totals by indicator may overlap and must not be added to count distinct phases.

Data and scope

Data
Seven tables: actual costs, actual durations, actual deliverables, project plans, project types, locations and country profiles.
Comparison level
A project and a phase, identified together by ProjectPhaseKey.
Indicators
Planned and actual costs, durations and deliverables; absolute and relative variances; phases and projects on alert.

The approach

  1. Clean all seven tables in Power Query: remove descriptive rows, promote the correct headers, fix data types and filter relevant missing values.
  2. Remove duplicates found in Project type and Projects_Locations, and keep only Region, Country and Type in Country_Profiles.
  3. Build ProjectPhaseKey from project ID and phase in the planned and actual tables. Keep Projects_plans as the reference at project-phase level.
  4. Define DAX variances. A relative variance at or above 15% triggers an alert. For deliverables, planned minus actual makes a shortfall positive.
  5. Count each phase only once in the distinct total, even if it triggers several types of alert.
  6. Organise an executive overview, region/country analysis and project-phase detail, with filters, country tooltips and security roles by scope.
  7. Document replacing the Excel extract, refreshing, updating a source path if needed, and checking errors, KPIs and filters.

Deliverables

An interactive Power BI report, a Product Strategy Canvas and documentation of the model and refresh process. The final presentation uses 10 slides for approximately 10 minutes, followed by a 5-minute Power BI demonstration.

The contribution

The project summary identifies costs as the first area to investigate and the Testing phase of IT projects as a point of concern. The recommendation is to strengthen budget estimates and checkpoints for those phases after examining the causes.

Findings and decisions

Alerts by indicator
214 cost, 159 schedule and 98 deliverable alerts are reported. A phase may appear in several categories, so these figures must not be added to count distinct phases.
Business focus
All 52 analysed D - Testing phases in IT projects are reported as having cost alerts, supporting a targeted investigation.
Access and maintenance
The France and Western Europe roles are described as tested using “View as”. The refresh procedure addresses a weekly update requirement.
DAX excerpt: count phases on alert

DAX · phase alert counting rule

Phases en alerte =
SUMX(
    VALUES(Projects_plans[ProjectPhaseKey]),
    IF(
        [Écart coût %] >= 0.15

The iteration uses distinct project-phase keys. The logical OR counts a phase once when at least one indicator reaches the threshold.

Limitations and perspective

Alert volume depends on variance definitions and the chosen threshold. An alert is a management signal, not proof of poor management or a causal explanation. The Q&A feature was explored but was not operational in the environment used.

Reference documents: the Sanitoral Power BI report and analysis summary. The report includes executive, region/country, project/phase, refresh, country tooltip and internal control views.

BottleNeck - Understand prices, margins and stock

How can management and online sales data provide a useful view of prices, margins and inventory?

PythonpandasExploratory analysisStatistics

The business need

BottleNeck has an ERP with 825 products, an 825-row mapping table and a 1,513-row web export. The study covers October sales and an inventory snapshot as of 31 October. The first challenge is to establish which references can be matched reliably.

714products matched to the web
€143,680October web revenue, rounded
31price outliers flagged using IQR

Results saved in the notebook and presentation. This is not total company revenue; inventory refers to a snapshot as of 31 October.

Data and scope

Three sources
ERP: prices, inventory and status; mapping table: product_id to SKU; web: product pages and sales.
Cleaned web data
716 product pages identified, with 2 missing SKUs isolated; 714 usable references with unique SKUs. Attachments and rows without useful content are excluded from sales analysis.
Matching
714 ERP products matched to the web; 111 without a usable match, including 91 missing SKUs and 20 mapping-table SKUs absent from the cleaned web data.

The approach

  1. Explore dimensions, data types, missing values and the role of each source.
  2. Isolate web product pages, harmonise identifiers and check uniqueness before joining.
  3. Consolidate ERP, mapping and web data while keeping track of unmatched references.
  4. Check selling and purchase prices, negative stock, inconsistent stock status and margins.
  5. Calculate revenue, inventory valuation and stock coverage, then examine revenue concentration and IQR price outliers.
  6. Calculate Pearson correlations and complement the coefficients with scatter plots.

Deliverables

An analytical notebook and a 14-page PDF presentation covering source diagnostics, matching, quality checks, commercial indicators, charts and recommendations for source systems.

The contribution

Priorities are to improve matching keys, check prices and stock status at source, and combine business and data quality indicators in future reporting.

Findings and decisions

ERP quality
3 zero or negative selling prices, 2 negative stock quantities, 2 quantity/status inconsistencies and 7 negative unit margins are identified.
Revenue concentration
434 products, or 60.78% of the analysed references, are needed to reach approximately 80% of revenue. The “80/20” rule is therefore not supported within this scope.
Price outliers
The upper IQR bound is €84.09. The 31 references above it are flagged for business review rather than removed automatically.
Inventory priorities
23 products sold with no available stock and 3 products with stock but no sales support targeted monitoring of stockouts and excess inventory.
Python excerpt: the correlation matrix

Python · excerpt from the BottleNeck notebook

df_correlation = df_analyse[variables_correlation].copy()

Pearson correlation describes a linear association and does not establish causality. Some variables are calculated from others, which must be considered when interpreting their relationship. A premium product can legitimately have an unusual price.

Data quality - Compare, then simplify

How can BottleNeck checks become clearer, more reproducible and easier to maintain before business analysis?

PythonpandasPandera · prototypeAI-assisted workflow

The business need

P6 already contains quality checks, but they are scattered. The P11 improvement adds a central validation step before analysis, without redesigning the KPIs or silently correcting data. Scoping, a technology review and two comparable prototypes precede the decision.

10 / 10rules matching the reference · pandas
10 / 10rules matching the reference · Pandera
10 + 120ERROR + WARNING flags in the notebook

All ten rules match the expected anomalies; this does not mean the data is error-free. A product may be flagged by more than one rule.

Data and scope

Data
Four prepared DataFrames: ERP, mapping table, web product pages and cleaned web data.
Protocol
The same ten rules and known anomalies are used as references for both pandas and Pandera.
Integrated solution
A valider_qualite_pandas() function returns a structured, quantified report with OK, ERROR or WARNING statuses.

The approach

  1. Define the need, stakeholders, scope boundaries and acceptance criteria.
  2. Review pandas, Pandera and Great Expectations, then prototype the first two approaches. Great Expectations remains a reviewed option.
  3. Compare the same ten rules for prices, inventory, margins and SKUs; assess coverage, data integrity, reproducibility and complexity.
  4. Select pandas for this notebook: observed coverage is equivalent, without adding a dependency to the P6 environment.
  5. Integrate validation immediately before business analysis and produce a summary of checks and flags.
  6. Document the use of AI for research and prototyping, the risks, the decision and a user guide.

Deliverables

An improved BottleNeck notebook and 21 pages of documentation covering scope, research, prototypes, decisions, risks, AI usage and a short user guide. The project also includes a PDF portfolio summary.

The contribution

The contribution is an explicit, reusable validation step before business calculations. The decision considers the need covered and maintenance effort, rather than assuming the most extensive tool is always the right choice.

Findings and decisions

Fair comparison
The documentation reports a 100% match against references for both prototypes, with unchanged data and reproducible results.
Technical decision
pandas is selected for ease of integration and no additional dependency. Pandera remains relevant if the scope grows.
Integrated function
Saved notebook outputs show 2 OK rules, 4 ERROR rules and 4 WARNING rules, with a reproducibility check returning True.
User support
The documentation explains how to run validation, read the report, investigate errors and warnings, and request source corrections. AI usage is documented.

Python · excerpt from the P11 notebook

rapport_qualite = valider_qualite_pandas(
    df_erp=df_erp_clean,

Limitations and perspective

The reference consists of known P6 anomalies, not an independent benchmark. Timings of around 0.01 and 0.03 seconds come from a small local trial and cannot be generalised. Validation does not block subsequent cells. No production pipeline or automatic correction is delivered.

Reference documents: improved P11 notebook, section 3 bis and saved results; documentation, pages 3 to 17 and user guide, pages 18 to 20. The documentation details the pandas/Pandera comparison and its limitations.
